Frequently Asked Questions

How does ResQAI prioritize emergency reports?
Our AI Risk Engine evaluates text description severity keywords, attached image diagnostics, citizen location proximity, and environmental weather data to assign an instant severity score (Critical, High, Medium, Low).
Can citizens report incidents without logging in?
Citizen reporting requires quick registration or single-click authentication so response teams can verify contact info, coordinate rescue locations, and send real-time SMS status updates.
How do government agencies access the command center?
Disaster Officers and Rescue Leads are assigned high-privilege JWT roles that unlock full resource allocation tools, emergency broadcast controls, and multi-agency GIS maps.
